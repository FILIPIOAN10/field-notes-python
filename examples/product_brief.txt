FocusFlow is a desktop app for knowledge workers that bundles notifications from Slack, email and Jira
into three scheduled digests per day (9:00, 13:00, 17:00) instead of real-time pings. Users can mark
up to five VIP contacts whose messages still come through instantly. A "deep work" timer blocks all
digests for 90 minutes and shows teammates a status in Slack.

Pricing: free for individuals; Teams plan at 6 EUR per user per month adds shared quiet hours,
an admin dashboard and weekly focus reports. The main risk the team sees is that users fear missing
urgent messages, so onboarding explains the VIP list and an "urgent" keyword override.

Planned features: calendar integration to auto-start deep work during blocked slots, AI-written
digest summaries, and a mobile companion app. Target customers are software teams of 10 to 200 people.
